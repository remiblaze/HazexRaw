# Hazex Raw — one-knob reverb space

![Hazex Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/HazexRaw/main/hazexraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob, from dry to a deep, hazy reverb space.**

Hazex Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Hazex** — the algorithmic stereo reverb. A single **SPACE** macro opens up an FDN reverb engine and moves size, decay, width, and mix together, level-matched so the sound never jumps in volume.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/HazexRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`HazexRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
- **SPACE** — the one knob. At zero the signal is clean and dry; turn it up and the sound drifts back into a lush, hazy algorithmic reverb that gets deeper and wider the further you push. The sweep is front-loaded — the first part adds a subtle room, and the top end is a wide, ambient wash. Size, decay, damping, stereo width, and dry/wet all move together, level-matched.

Drop it on vocals, drums, synths, a pad, or a full bus and open the one knob. A pure audio insert — no menus, no setup.

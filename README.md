# Hazex Raw: one-knob reverb space

![Hazex Raw free one-knob reverb UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/HazexRaw/main/hazexraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob, from dry to a deep, hazy reverb space.**

Hazex Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Hazex**, the algorithmic stereo reverb. A single **SPACE** macro opens up an FDN reverb engine and moves size, decay, width, and mix together, level-matched so the sound never jumps in volume.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/HazexRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`HazexRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Hazex Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`HazexRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Hazex Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
- **SPACE**: the one knob. At zero the signal is clean and dry; turn it up and the sound drifts back into a lush, hazy algorithmic reverb that gets deeper and wider the further you push. The sweep is front-loaded, the first part adds a subtle room, and the top end is a wide, ambient wash. Size, decay, damping, stereo width, and dry/wet all move together, level-matched.

Drop it on vocals, drums, synths, a pad, or a full bus and open the one knob. A pure audio insert, no menus, no setup.
